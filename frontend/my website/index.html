About Us

The Daily Grind is a cozy coffee shop nestled in the heart of Pune. We are passionate about serving the highest quality coffee, made from carefully selected beans. Our friendly baristas are dedicated to crafting the perfect cup for every customer, whether you prefer a classic espresso or a unique specialty drink.

We believe in creating a welcoming atmosphere where you can relax, work, or catch up with friends. Come and experience the difference!
